## Permanent account deletion semantics

SUPER_ADMIN can permanently delete an archived account only when it has zero transactions and its latest effective opening baseline is zero. With no transactions, latest override equals effective opening; an earlier non-zero override alone no longer blocks.

- Same-month non-zero override later cleared to automatic zero: deletable; audit stays.
- Earlier non-zero override followed by newer zero: deletable; retain both baseline rows and all audit, keeping historical balances unchanged.
- Only zero overrides: they may be cleaned up while deleting the master account; audit stays.
- Any transaction, non-zero latest baseline, unarchived account or non-SUPER_ADMIN role: blocked.
- Audited account names cannot be reused through create/rename, preventing historical financial rows from leaking into a new master account.

Do not cascade-delete transactions, historical non-zero values or audit, and do not recreate wrapper/version owners.
